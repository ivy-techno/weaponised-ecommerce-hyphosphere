# Hyphosphere — Devpost demonstration script

Target length: **2 minutes 35 seconds**. The rules cap the video at three minutes. Record at 100% zoom from the final public URL in a WebMCP-compatible browser, with narration and only original or properly licensed audio.

## 0:00–0:20 — The research problem

**Show:** Homepage title, thesis proof-of-concept introduction, and research-area ribbon.

**Say:** “Information operations are normally encountered as posts, adverts, or takedowns. But those are only the visible surface. Hyphosphere investigates the commercial services, datasets, platforms, interfaces, and infrastructure that help an operation travel, scale, or persist.”

## 0:20–0:42 — A reproducible investigation

**Show:** Open **Investigation paths**, begin the Atlas Relay example, and follow one relationship.

**Say:** “The visitor starts with a reproducible example. Two cases appear to share an ordinary service. Following the relationship reveals possible supporting material, but Hyphosphere preserves the distinction between verified, supported, inferred, and disputed evidence.”

## 0:42–1:02 — Human and agent share the interface

**Show:** In the agent conversation, invoke `get_evidence` for Atlas Relay. Capture the evidence drawer visibly opening on the page. Then invoke `set_evidence_threshold` and show the visible filtering response.

**Say:** “WebMCP gives the agent the same inspectable surface as the researcher. Here the agent opens evidence and changes the threshold through registered page tools. The action is visible and reversible; the human still decides what the evidence means.”

## 1:02–1:27 — Search across the ecommerce stack

**Show:** Invoke `run_research_brief` with a focused query, scope `both`, and one stack layer. Review the candidates that appear on screen and retain one.

**Say:** “The agent can search the curated research terrain and linked source index by ecommerce-stack position, then assemble a small candidate brief. Direct citations and inclusion reasons remain attached, so assistance does not become an unexplained answer.”

## 1:27–1:52 — Agent-mediated current signals

**Show:** Invoke `publish_signal_feed` with two current, HTTPS-linked examples. Let the feed move, pause it, open one citation, and save one lead.

**Say:** “For current material, the agent supplies link-backed headlines to a visible signal feed, with an information-warfare label, stack position, and relevance note. The researcher can pause the flow, inspect the original source, and choose what deserves retention.”

## 1:52–2:15 — Build a reusable notebook

**Show:** Open **Saved notebook**. Display the saved lead beside the retained brief and discovery. If available, show Markdown export.

**Say:** “The outcome is not another feed of troll activity. It is a researcher-controlled notebook that can compare live leads with existing artifacts, preserve uncertainty, and support later writing, timelines, network graphics, or case studies.”

## 2:15–2:35 — Why WebMCP

**Show:** Return to the research-area ribbon and briefly show Source ecology and Possible outputs.

**Say:** “This is a first glimpse of a larger online observatory. WebMCP is the bridge: agents can gather, structure, and operate the terrain, while people retain agency over provenance, interpretation, and what becomes part of the record.”
